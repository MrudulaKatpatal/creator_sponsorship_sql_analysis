# Data Dictionary

## platforms
Stores supported social media platforms.

## creators
Stores creator profile information such as niche, city, and join date.

## brands
Stores brands and industries.

## creator_accounts
Connects creators to platforms and stores follower count and engagement rate.

## campaigns
Stores campaign details, budgets, objectives, and dates.

## campaign_creators
Bridge table connecting campaigns and creators, including agreed fee and status.

## content_posts
Stores sponsored content performance: impressions, likes, comments, clicks, conversions, and revenue.

## payments
Stores payment amounts, dates, and payment status for creator assignments.
